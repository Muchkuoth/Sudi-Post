Short version

We collect what we need to move a parcel and invoice you for it. We do not sell your data. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

This is a demonstration policy. It describes how a Sudi-Post deployment is intended to work. Before going live, have counsel review it against the South Sudanese, Ethiopian and any other data protection law that applies to your operation.

1. Who we are

Sudi-Post Delivery Services ("Sudi-Post", "we", "us") operates a private postal, courier and logistics network connecting South Sudan and Ethiopia. Sudi-Post is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

Registered address: Malakia Road, Juba, South Sudan. Ethiopian operations are administered from Bole Sub-City, Woreda 03, Addis Ababa.

2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to do the job, and we ask for it at the moment we need it.

2.1 Information you give us

  • Sender details — name, phone number, email address, pickup address and city.
  • Recipient details — name, phone number, delivery address and city. We do not require the recipient's email or identity documents for standard deliveries.
  • Shipment contents — a short description you provide, weight, dimensions, and the declared value where you ask for insurance.
  • Account details — name, email, phone, city and, for business accounts, company name and any billing information you add.
  • Payment details — the method used and a transaction reference. Card numbers are entered directly into the payment provider's hosted fields and are never stored by Sudi-Post.
  • Correspondence — messages you send through the contact, business or enquiry forms, including your email address and anything you write.

2.2 Information generated by using our services

  • Shipment reference, status history, timestamps and scanned location events.
  • Proof of delivery: recipient name, signature image, delivery photo and time.
  • Invoice records, receipts, credit notes and payment status.
  • Pickup requests, saved addresses and your notification preferences.
  • Basic technical data: browser type, device type, and the pages you visited.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect special-category personal data, we do not ask for national identity numbers as a condition of delivery, and we do not record the contents of a parcel beyond the description you give us.

3. How we use it

  • To perform our contract with you — collecting, routing, delivering and handing proof of delivery for your shipment.
  • To communicate about your shipment — status alerts by SMS, WhatsApp or email, and notifications when a delivery is attempted or fails.
  • To invoice you correctly — calculating freight, surcharges and any declared-value cover, then collecting payment.
  • To keep the network reliable — planning capacity, measuring on-time performance and investigating lost or delayed parcels.
  • To meet legal obligations — customs and tax records for cross-border movements, and any record-keeping law that applies to us.
  • To improve the service — in aggregate, such as which routes carry the most volume. These reports are not linked to an individual.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on the following:

  • Performance of a contract — for everything needed to deliver your parcel and bill you for it. This covers the majority of our processing.
  • Legal obligation — for customs declarations, tax records and statutory books.
  • Legitimate interests — for fraud prevention, network security, and improving routes. We balance these against your rights and stop where the impact is too high.
  • Consent — for marketing email and, where we rely on it, optional cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out.

5. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary, and only with parties that need it to serve you:

  • Delivery agents — the recipient's name, address, phone number and the parcel reference for stops on their manifest. Agents see only their own stops.
  • Payment providers — the amount, currency and reference, so they can process your payment. They act as independent controllers for that transaction.
  • Customs and border authorities — where a cross-border movement requires a declaration.
  • Partner pickup points — the sender's address, so a partner can collect on our behalf. They are bound by the same obligations.
  • Suppliers and professional advisers — IT hosting, SMS delivery and accounting, under contract and only on our instructions.

We do not sell your data, and we do not disclose recipient details to the sender beyond what tracking already shows.

6. Payments

Card details are entered on the payment provider's own secure page. Sudi-Post never receives or stores a full card number, and never sees your PIN or mobile money PIN.

We keep the method, the amount, the currency, the provider's transaction reference and the date, because we need them for your receipt, for reconciliation and for dispute handling.

Cash on delivery means the recipient settles the freight with our agent. In that case no payment data passes through us at all.

7. How long we keep it

  • Shipment records and proof of delivery24 months from delivery
  • Invoices and receipts7 years (statutory accounting requirement)
  • Customs and cross-border declarationsAs required by the relevant authority
  • Account detailsUntil you close the account, then 12 months
  • Correspondence24 months from last contact
  • Technical logs90 days

After these periods we delete or irreversibly anonymise the data. Where we must keep something longer for a legal reason, we restrict it to that purpose and tell you if you ask.

8. Your rights

Wherever we operate, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold — a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it — fix anything inaccurate, from your account or by asking us.
  • Delete it — erase data we no longer have a legal reason to keep.
  • Restrict or object to processing — for example, if you believe a legitimate interest assessment is wrong.
  • Receive it in a portable format — your shipments, invoices and addresses as CSV or JSON.
  • Withdraw consent — for marketing, at any time, using the unsubscribe link or by telling us.
  • Complain — to us first, and then to the relevant data protection authority in your country.

We respond within 30 days and we do not charge for a reasonable request. We may ask you to confirm your identity so we do not disclose your data to the wrong person.

9. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit on every public page and API call.
  • Role-based access: agents see their own manifests, staff see what their role requires, and access is logged.
  • Signed delivery events recorded with the time, the agent and the device.
  • Regular credential rotation and a documented incident response process.
  • Staff confidentiality terms in every employment contract.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data and creates a meaningful risk, we will notify you and the relevant authority without undue delay.

10. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising cookies and does not embed third-party trackers, map services or social media scripts.

Your browser's local storage is used to keep you signed in, to remember your saved addresses and preferences, and to hold the state of a form you have started. That data stays on your device and is cleared when you clear site data or use the "Reset local data" control in your account.

Clearing your browser data means you will need to sign in again and re-enter anything you had not already sent to us.

11. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Where a parcel is addressed to a child, the sender is the contracting party and is responsible for the accuracy of the address.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our processing changes. The effective date at the top always shows the current version. Where a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you by email or on this page before it takes effect.

Earlier versions are available on request.
