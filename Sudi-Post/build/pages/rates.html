How the price is worked out

  • One fixed rate. 1 kilogram always costs 23,000 SSP, on every route we serve.
  • Weight times rate. Your total is simply your weight in kilograms multiplied by 23,000.
  • Part kilograms count. 3.5 kg is charged as 3.5 kg, so 80,500 SSP.
  • No extras added. The calculator total is the amount you actually pay.

Payment

We only mark a payment Paid when the money arrives

Pressing Pay Now starts a payment. It does not mark your invoice as paid, and it does not mark it as failed either. Only the payment provider's confirmation moves the status.

    Why it works this way. A payment is only successful when money actually reaches us. If you close the payment page or your network drops, the status stays Pending and nothing is taken from you — you can safely pay again later using the same reference.
