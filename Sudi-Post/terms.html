This is a demonstration document. It is written to be complete and readable, but it has not been through legal review. Before you trade on these terms, have counsel adapt them to your registered entity and the jurisdictions you actually operate in.

1. Acceptance of these terms

These terms form a binding agreement between you and Sudi-Post Delivery Services ("Sudi-Post") when you book a shipment, register an account, or otherwise ask us to carry something.

By using this website or our services you confirm that you have read and accept these terms. If you do not accept them, do not use the service. If you are accepting on behalf of an organisation, you confirm that you have authority to bind that organisation.

2. Definitions

  • "Sender" — the person or organisation handing goods to us.
  • "Recipient" — the person or organisation named to receive goods.
  • "Consignment" — one shipment booked under one tracking reference.
  • "Chargeable weight" — the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight (length × width × height in cm ÷ 5000 kg), with a 0.5 kg minimum.
  • "Business Day" — Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays in the country of collection.
  • "Transit time" — the estimated working time from acceptance at our facility to delivery, published in our rate card for the relevant zone and service.
  • "SLA" — a service level commitment, where one has been agreed in writing.

3. Our services

Sudi-Post provides private postal, courier and logistics services between and within South Sudan and Ethiopia, including:

  • Document and parcel delivery on scheduled and express services.
  • Business and bulk shipments with scheduled collections and consolidated invoicing.
  • Cash on delivery and declared-value cover.
  • E-commerce fulfilment support, including returns.

We may add, change or withdraw a service. Where a change affects a service you have already booked, we will tell you before it takes effect.

4. Acceptable items

You may send ordinary goods and documents that are legal to possess and transport in both South Sudan and Ethiopia, and that will not cause harm if handled normally.

The sender is responsible for the accuracy of the description, the weight, the dimensions and the declared value. If a parcel is misdescribed and turns out to need different handling, we may re-rate it and charge the difference, or refuse it.

5. Prohibited items

We will not accept, and you must not tender:

  • Illicit drugs, controlled substances and their precursors.
  • Firearms, ammunition and explosives, except under a valid permit and with prior written approval.
  • Currency, negotiable instruments and high-value bullion above the declared threshold without prior written approval.
  • Live animals, and animal products without the required veterinary documentation.
  • Perishable goods that require temperature control, unless a refrigerated service has been agreed in writing.
  • Counterfeit, stolen or infringing goods, or anything the subject of a customs hold.
  • Hazardous materials, including most batteries shipped loose, fuels, aerosols above the permitted limit, and corrosive or toxic substances.
  • Human remains, and any shipment you are not legally entitled to send or receive.

Where we reasonably believe an item falls into this list, we may refuse it, hold it, and notify the relevant authority. We will not be liable for loss or delay arising from a refusal made in good faith.

6. Quotes and charges

  • Every quote is calculated on chargeable weight, package type, service level, zone and any surcharges you select.
  • The quote shown on this website is an estimate valid for 7 days. The binding price is the one on your booking confirmation or invoice.
  • Where the actual weight or dimensions differ from those declared, we re-rate the consignment and charge the difference before delivery.
  • Charges are quoted in South Sudanese pounds unless the invoice states otherwise. Where we quote a USD equivalent, it is indicative and the SSP figure governs.
  • A service fee of 5% applies to cover handling, sorting and proof of delivery. It is shown separately in the breakdown.
  • Customs duty, import tax and any clearance fee are the recipient's responsibility unless your agreement with us says otherwise.

If we make an obvious pricing error and you have acted reasonably on it, we will honour the quoted price. We will not normally charge you more than 10% above the confirmed price on a consignment we have already accepted.

7. Booking and collection

  • A booking is confirmed when we issue a tracking reference. An on-screen quote is not a booking.
  • You must ensure someone is available at the collection address during the agreed window.
  • Where we cannot collect on the first attempt, we will contact you. Repeated failed collections may be charged at the applicable rate and may lead to the consignment being returned.
  • Please package items securely. We are not liable for damage caused by inadequate packaging.
  • Collection cut-offs and scheduled departures are published on our routes page. Loading after cut-off moves the consignment to the next departure.

8. Delivery

  • Transit times are estimates in working days and exclude weekends at the destination, public holidays, and customs clearance.
  • Delivery is attempted up to three times. After that the consignment is returned to the sending branch and the return fee applies.
  • If nobody is available we may deliver to a neighbour, a reception, or a safe place nominated by the recipient, at our discretion. We do not open parcels.
  • A delivery is evidenced by proof of delivery: recipient name, signature, time and, where requested, a photograph.
  • Where the recipient refuses a parcel, the freight and any charges already incurred remain due.
  • We may hold a consignment pending payment of duties, or pending a customs query, and we will tell you as soon as we can.

Where an SLA applies, our on-time figure and any resulting credit are calculated monthly against the SLA in your written agreement, and credits are applied automatically.

9. Accounts, credit and payment

  • You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and for everything done through your account.
  • Tell us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.
  • Payment is due on delivery for cash-on-delivery accounts, and within the stated terms for invoiced accounts.
  • Overdue invoices accrue interest at 2% per month from the due date, where permitted by law.
  • We may suspend collection or delivery on consignments where an invoice is more than 30 days overdue, after giving you written notice.
  • Bank details are never changed by email. If you receive a message claiming our account has changed, call us on the number on this website before sending anything.

10. Liability

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited.

Subject to that:

  • We are liable for the loss of or damage to goods in our custody, up to the declared value where you have bought cover, and up to the consignment charge where you have not.
  • We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost profit, lost business opportunity or loss of data, however arising.
  • We are not liable for delay or failure caused by events outside our reasonable control, which is dealt with in clause 13.
  • Our total aggregate liability in any twelve-month period is limited to the total charges you paid us in that period.
  • You must tell us about damage or shortage within 14 days of delivery with photographs of the packaging and contents. We may not be liable for claims raised later.

We are not liable for the contents of a consignment, for its commercial value beyond the declared amount, or for how the recipient uses what we deliver.

11. Claims

  • Raise a claim through our contact page or your account, quoting the tracking reference.
  • We aim to acknowledge within one business day and to resolve within 15 business days.
  • Where we agree that a claim is valid, we will repair, replace or refund as appropriate, and where an SLA credit applies it is applied automatically.
  • Claims for concealed damage need photographs of the outer packaging, the internal packaging and the damaged item.

12. Insurance and declared value

  • Declared value is your statement of the value of the goods. It is not insurance.
  • Insurance cover is charged at 2% of the declared value and covers loss or damage in transit, up to the amount declared.
  • Without insurance, our liability is limited to the consignment charge.
  • We do not cover jewellery, precious metals, cash, negotiable instruments, artworks, or perishable goods, even when declared.
  • Declared value above USD 5,000 requires prior written approval.

13. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for failure to perform caused by an event beyond its reasonable control, including war, civil unrest, epidemic, flood, road closure, border closure, strike, government action, or the failure of a public utility or telecommunications network.

Where such an event affects a consignment, we will tell you as soon as we reasonably can and reschedule or reroute where possible. If the consignment cannot be delivered within 60 days, we will refund the freight charge, and that is our sole obligation in respect of that consignment.

14. Complaints

If something has gone wrong, we would rather hear it early.

  • Raise it with your named account contact, or through the contact page.
  • We will acknowledge within one business day and give you a named person who owns the complaint.
  • If you are not satisfied, ask for escalation. The escalation is reviewed by a manager who is not involved in the original handling.
  • You also have the right to take the matter to the relevant consumer or regulatory body in your country.

15. Suspension and termination

We may suspend or terminate an account, or refuse a consignment, where we reasonably believe that:

  • the account is being used fraudulently or in breach of these terms;
  • the consignment contains a prohibited item;
  • invoices are more than 60 days overdue;
  • continued service would breach a law or a customs instruction;
  • a consignment presents a safety risk to our people or our network.

Where we suspend for the account holder's benefit, we refund any unearned charges. We will give notice and an opportunity to put things right unless doing so would be unlawful or impractical.

You may close your account at any time. Consignments already in our care remain subject to these terms until they are delivered or returned.
